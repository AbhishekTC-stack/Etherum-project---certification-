How it works

1. The institution (admin) signs in with its wallet and issues a certificate.
2. The certificate record is stored permanently on the Ethereum blockchain.
3. Anyone can verify it using the certificate ID. The result comes straight from the blockchain, so it cannot be faked or edited later.
